⚠ The Crisis We Solve
When disaster strikes, survivors face five compounding failures —
failures that keep them trapped long after the headlines fade.
🏚️
26M+
Displaced Annually
Natural disasters displace over 26 million people every year. Temporary shelters are not homes — they lack privacy, cultural accommodation, and dignity.
⏳
2–5 yrs
Rebuild Wait Time
Traditional construction takes years post-disaster. Middle-income survivors drain savings paying for hotels and rentals while waiting — unable to resume their livelihoods.
🗑️
Millions
Tons of Debris Unprocessed
Post-disaster sites are buried under rubble. This debris is hazardous, expensive to remove, and blocks access to land — yet it is treated as pure waste.
💸
×3–5
Cost Surge in Materials
Importing new construction materials into a disaster zone is expensive and slow. Supply chains are disrupted. Costs multiply. Reconstruction stalls.
💔

Design Dignity in Shelters
Cookie-cutter shelters ignore religious spaces, family layouts, and cultural needs. Survivors lose not just their home — but their identity and sense of belonging.
📍
Can't
Just "Move Away"
Jobs, farmland, community, and social networks are rooted in the affected region. Displacement means permanent economic destruction — not just temporary inconvenience.
"Survivors don't just need shelter — they need their home, their community, and their future back."
⚙ Our Three-Way Solution
ReBuild3D closes all five gaps in one integrated pipeline —
turning the disaster site's own ruins into permanent, dignified homes.
💥 STEP 1
Disaster Site
Rubble, concrete, and structural debris collected from the affected area
🤖 STEP 2
AI Material Analysis
AI scans and classifies debris composition to find the optimal filament mix
♻️ STEP 3
Filament Conversion
Ruins processed and converted into printable construction-grade filament
🖨️ STEP 4
3D Printing
Large-scale 3D printers construct 1–4 story structures in a fraction of the time
🏠 STEP 5
Your Home, Your Design
App-based CAD library — survivors choose their layout, culture, and needs
🔄
Debris Becomes the Feedstock

By sourcing filament directly from disaster rubble, we simultaneously clean the site and eliminate raw material import costs — a self-funding supply chain born from destruction.

📱
Dignity Through Design Choice

Our CAD app gives survivors miniature previews of their homes. Prayer rooms, family layouts, cultural architecture — chosen by the people living in them, not by relief organizations.

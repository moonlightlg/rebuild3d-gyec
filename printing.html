Modular & Portable

Built from 2.5 m gantry modules that ship in standard containers and re-assemble on-site. Scales from a single house to a four-storey block by adding modules — critical for unpredictable disaster footprints.

🌍

Field-Proven Humanitarian Use

Already deployed in Malawi, Kenya, Madagascar, USA Habitat for Humanity, and more. The only construction printer with a documented track record in low-infrastructure environments.

🔓

Open Material System

Unlike closed-recipe competitors, COBOD accepts third-party concrete mixes — including our RCA-based filament. The hardware does not lock us into proprietary feedstock.

⚙ The Machine

An overhead gantry moves a print head across the build envelope —
laying down our RCA concrete in 10–25 mm layers, up to 1 m/s.

▸ The Print Workflow
1
📱 Survivor Selects Design
CAD library app — survivor browses culturally-appropriate layouts (prayer room, family configuration, plot fit) and previews a miniature.
2
✂ Slicing → G-code
CAD model is sliced into 10–25 mm horizontal layers; toolpaths and extrusion rates compiled to printer G-code.
3
🧪 RCA Mix Loaded
Mixer is charged with our SSD-saturated FRCA, blended binder, and admixtures. AI verifies recipe against the current batch.
4
🖨 Print
Gantry traverses on rails; concrete is pumped to the print head and extruded layer-by-layer. Accelerator dosed at nozzle for instant buildability.
5
🔩 Rebar Insertion
Workers place vertical/horizontal reinforcement bars between layers at programmed pauses — bridging the only step COBOD does not automate.
6
⏲ Cure & Finish
Walls cure on-site; doors, windows, roof, and services are fitted. Total shell time: typically < 4 weeks for a small home.

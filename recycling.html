From Ruins to Filament

The ReBuild3D Recycled Cement Aggregate (RCA) Pipeline
Process
02
⚠ STEP 0
HAZMAT SCREENING — done before crushing begins.
Pre-1980s structures often contain asbestos, lead paint, and PCBs. Trained crews identify and divert contaminated debris (UNDP Debris-Management SOP), protecting workers and downstream concrete quality.
1
🔨Primary Crushing
A jaw or impact crusher reduces large structural debris — slabs, columns, masonry — from field size down to manageable fragments. This is the bottleneck stage; throughput here governs the rest of the line.
Input: 300–500 mm → Output: 50–100 mm
▼
2
🧲Magnetic Separation
An overband electromagnet lifts ferrous metals — rebar, steel mesh, structural anchors — off the conveyor. Essential: rebar would destroy downstream crushers.
Ferrous removal >95%
3
⚡Eddy Current Separation
A rotating high-frequency magnetic field repels non-ferrous metals — copper wiring, aluminium framing — that magnets miss. Prevents long-term electrochemical corrosion in the printed structure.
Non-ferrous removal >90%
▼
4
📊Vibrating Sieve · Stage 1
Oscillating screen decks classify by size. Oversized fragments loop back to the crusher; fine dust (<0.075 mm) is set aside as supplementary filler for the mix.
Pass band: ~5–50 mm to next stage
▼
5
💨Air Jigging — Density Separation
Pulsed upward airflow through a perforated deck stratifies particles by density. Heavy concrete & ceramic sink and are kept; lighter wood, plastics, and gypsum rise and are diverted. This is the step that converts low-grade C&D waste into structural-grade feedstock.

6
⚙Secondary Impact Crusher
Specifically an impact crusher — not jaw — because impact produces cubical particles. Cubical aggregate packs denser, demands less water, and prints more predictably than elongated jaw output.
Output: 0–10 mm, cubical shape
7
📐Vibrating Sieve · Stage 2
Final gradation. Targets fine RCA (FRCA) at 0–5 mm — the rule of thumb of 1/3 of nozzle diameter (~25–40 mm at COBOD), with safety margin for pumpability.
Spec: 0–5 mm FRCA
▼
8
💧SSD Pre-Wetting (Saturated Surface Dry)
RCA absorbs 3–10% water — five to ten times more than virgin aggregate. Without correction, the aggregate steals mix water and the effective water-to-cement ratio rises, weakening the print. Pre-saturating to SSD locks absorption before mixing.
Untreated RCA → up to 30% strength loss
▼
9
🧪Mix Engineering — 40 : 60 Binder : Aggregate
Binder is not pure cement: OPC is partially replaced by silica fume or fly ash, which densifies the paste–aggregate interface and recovers the strength lost to RCA. Three admixtures govern printability: superplasticizer (flow), retarder (open time), accelerator (buildability at the nozzle).
Compressive target: ≥25 MPa (residential code)

10
🤖AI Quality Control — The Adaptive Layer
Disaster debris composition varies street by street. A vision-and-spectroscopy model classifies each batch, measures water absorption, predicts compressive strength, and outputs a corrected recipe in real time. This is what makes the pipeline robust to chaotic input — and what differentiates ReBuild3D from a fixed-recipe printer.
Closed-loop adjustment of water, SCM ratio, admixture dose
